## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Upstream network → local artifact/source | Mutable metadata or substituted binaries could alter discovery subject. |
| Stock Pyramid → workstation | Young binary writes state/logs and could bind beyond loopback. |
| Audit/tracer → private journal/Git | Raw operational detail must be retained privately without repository leakage. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01-01 | Tampering | `config/pyramid.lock`, verifier | high | mitigate | Canonical URL, execution-time full commit/tag/size/SHA-256, clean tagged source reproduction, immutable artifact selection. |
| T-01-02 | Elevation of Privilege | local Pyramid listener | high | mitigate | Fresh marked state, loopback assertion, disposable identity, timeout, exit cleanup, no privileged signer. |
| T-01-03 | Information Disclosure | journal/repository boundary | high | mitigate | Journal-first outside-worktree checks, restrictive modes, raw captures private, narrow tracked/staged preflight, value-free diagnostics. |
| T-01-04 | Denial of Service | local tracer | low | accept | One bounded disposable process with timeout and cleanup; residual workstation load is limited and observable. |
| T-01-05 | Tampering | NIP-86 coverage | medium | mitigate | Manual three-way execution-time comparison with immutable private journal evidence and reviewed INTEGRATE/OPT-OUT rows. |
